How this app gets its numbers (how)

• Each price is checked against at least two independent reports. The badges show the result: verified two sources agree; single source only one could be confirmed; stale the latest published value is old.

• Websites that disagree with the IBJA benchmark by more than 1% are ignored. Live feeds are cross-checked too: on 20 Nov one data feed was 2.5% off the market and was rejected.

• The next-days ranges come from Cboe's options-market volatility indices (GVZ for gold, VXSLV for silver). They show how far prices may move, not which direction.

• India rates are IBJA benchmark rates. They exclude 3% GST and making charges, so your jeweller's bill will be higher.

• If the data is more than a day old, the header shows a warning.
